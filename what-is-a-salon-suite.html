What Is a Salon Suite?

A salon suite is a private, fully enclosed studio space where independent beauty and personal care professionals can run their own business. Unlike chair rental or booth rental, a salon suite gives you your own room with a lockable door, full control of your brand, and a premium environment for serving clients.

How salon suites work

In a salon suite model, a professional rents or holds a membership for an individual, private room inside a larger facility. Each suite functions as its own mini-salon or spa. The professional controls everything inside their suite: the decor, the products they use, the prices they charge, and the hours they work.

At NOVA Collective in Louisville, KY, we use a membership model rather than a traditional lease. This means lower commitment, more flexibility, and a community-focused approach. Your monthly membership dues give you access to your private suite, shared amenities, and the collective energy of a curated group of professionals.

Salon suite vs. chair rental: what is the difference?

Many beauty professionals start by renting a chair or booth in someone else's salon. While this works early in your career, it has significant limitations as your business grows.

Privacy

Chair rental: You share an open floor with other stylists. Clients can see and hear everything. Salon suite: Your own enclosed room with a door. Total privacy for you and your clients.

Branding

Chair rental: You work under the salon owner's brand and rules. Salon suite: Your name, your aesthetic, your products, your service menu. Everything reflects your brand.

Schedule

Chair rental: Often bound by salon hours and shift requirements. Salon suite: Complete control over your hours. Book clients when it works for you and your lifestyle.

Revenue

Chair rental: Commission splits or high weekly chair fees. Salon suite: Keep 100% of your service and retail revenue. Predictable monthly membership dues.

Customization

Chair rental: Limited to your chair area. Cannot change decor or layout. Salon suite: Furnish and design your space however you want. It is your studio.

Growth

Chair rental: Hard to build a business asset. Salon suite: Build a brand, develop client loyalty, grow retail sales, and create a business that has real value.

Who should consider a salon suite?

Salon suites are ideal for established beauty and personal care professionals who have an existing client base and want to take full control of their business. Common professionals who thrive in a salon suite include:

Hair Stylists

Stylists who want to create a signature salon experience and keep 100% of their revenue.

Barbers

Barbers ready for their own private shop space without the overhead of a full storefront.

Estheticians

Skincare professionals who need a calm, private treatment room for facials and spa services.

Nail Technicians

Nail techs who want their own elegant workspace for manicures, pedicures, and nail art.

Massage Therapists

Licensed massage therapists who need a quiet, professional room for bodywork and relaxation services.

Tattoo Artists

Tattoo artists who want a clean, private studio with full control over their workspace.

Lash Technicians

Lash and brow pros who need a quiet, focused room for detailed extension and shaping work.

Wellness Professionals

Reiki practitioners, waxing specialists, makeup artists, and other personal care providers.
