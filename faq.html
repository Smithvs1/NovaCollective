Frequently Asked Questions About Salon Suites in Louisville, KY

Everything you need to know about NOVA Collective, private salon suites, memberships, pricing, and how to apply.

What is a salon suite?

A salon suite is a private, fully enclosed studio space where independent beauty and personal care professionals run their own business. Unlike chair rental or booth rental, you get your own lockable room with full control over your brand, schedule, products, and pricing.

NOVA Collective offers premium salon suites in Louisville, KY for hair stylists, barbers, estheticians, nail techs, massage therapists, tattoo artists, and lash technicians.

NOVA Collective is currently evaluating high-visibility Louisville neighborhoods with central access, client-friendly parking, and a premium atmosphere. The exact address will be announced to Founding Members first.

How much does a salon suite cost at NOVA Collective?

NOVA Collective uses a membership model with predictable monthly dues instead of a traditional lease. Founding Members receive early, locked-in pricing. A fully refundable $500 deposit is required to reserve your spot in the Founding Member selection process.

Specific monthly rates will be shared with approved applicants.

What is the $500 deposit and is it refundable?

The $500 Founding Member deposit reserves your place in the selection process for NOVA Collective salon suites. It is fully refundable at any time before your move-in date, no questions asked. If you change your mind, simply reach out and your deposit will be returned in full.

What types of professionals can join NOVA Collective?

NOVA Collective serves independent beauty, wellness, and personal care professionals including:

  • Hair stylists
  • Barbers
  • Estheticians
  • Nail technicians
  • Massage therapists
  • Lash technicians
  • Tattoo artists
  • Reiki practitioners, makeup artists, waxing specialists, and other personal care providers

Is NOVA Collective a lease or a membership?

NOVA Collective operates on a membership model through a Master Membership Agreement, which is a revocable license to use the space. This is not a traditional lease. The membership model gives you more flexibility and fewer long-term commitments compared to a standard commercial lease.

Can I customize my salon suite?

Yes. NOVA Collective members can customize their private suite with their own furnishings, decor, paint colors, lighting, signage, and equipment. Your suite should reflect your brand and professional identity.

How do I apply for a salon suite at NOVA Collective?

Visit the Apply page on our website and complete the professional application form. We review each application to ensure a strong fit for the collective. Approved applicants will receive a private link to complete their $500 refundable deposit.

When will NOVA Collective open in Louisville?

The full process from securing the building to grand opening typically takes 6 to 10 months. This includes:

  • Pre-construction phase: property search, lease negotiation, and permitting (3-6 months)
  • Build-out phase: framing, plumbing, electrical, and finishes (3-4 months)

Founding Members will receive a firm move-in estimate 60 to 90 days before opening.

Do I need my own clients to join NOVA Collective?

NOVA Collective is designed for established professionals with an existing client base. Ideal candidates are independent beauty, wellness, and personal care professionals who have a clear standard for client experience and are ready to operate their own private studio.

What are the differences between a salon suite and chair rental?

A salon suite gives you a private, lockable room with full brand control, your own schedule, no commission splits, and the ability to customize your space. Chair rental means sharing an open floor, working under someone else's brand, limited personalization, and often paying commissions or high weekly fees.
