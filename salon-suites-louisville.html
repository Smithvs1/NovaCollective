Private Salon Suites

Your own private beauty studio in Louisville

Whether you are a hair stylist, barber, esthetician, nail tech, massage therapist, lash tech, or tattoo artist, NOVA Collective gives you a private, fully customizable suite in a premium Louisville location.

Unlike traditional chair rental or booth rental, a NOVA Collective salon suite is your own private workspace. You control the schedule, the branding, the products, and the client experience. It is a membership, not a lease, giving you the flexibility and independence you need to grow your business.
