Why hair stylists choose a private salon suite

As a hair stylist, your clients expect a premium, personal experience. In a traditional salon, you share space, split commissions, and work under someone else's brand. A private salon suite at NOVA Collective changes all of that.

At NOVA Collective, your suite is part of a membership-based collective — not a traditional lease. You get the independence of your own private studio with the support and energy of a curated professional community in Louisville.
