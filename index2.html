The Club Experience

Independence is respected. Community is built in.

NOVA Collective is a modern personal care club built for beauty professionals, massage therapists, nail techs, and tattoo artists ready to evolve beyond the freelance grind and step into their own business.

Our private pods are intentionally designed to feel like individual studios—defined, elevated, and fully yours—while still preserving the connection, culture, and shared energy that fuels both artists and clients.

This creates an environment where professionals can grow their brand, serve clients with purpose, and thrive within a community of like-minded creatives.

For clients, NOVA offers the best of both worlds: the intimacy of a one-on-one experience paired with the atmosphere of an upscale personal care destination.

At NOVA, every artist has the space to shine.
