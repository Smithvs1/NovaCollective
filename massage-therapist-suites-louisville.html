Your own private massage therapy suite in Louisville, Kentucky. NOVA Collective gives licensed massage therapists and bodywork professionals a calm, professional treatment room in a premium setting.

Why massage therapists choose a private suite

Your clients come to you for relaxation, pain relief, and healing. The environment matters. A private massage therapy suite at NOVA Collective eliminates the noise, interruptions, and compromises of shared spaces.

At NOVA Collective, your suite is part of a membership-based collective — not a traditional lease. You get the independence of your own private studio with the support and energy of a curated professional community in Louisville.
