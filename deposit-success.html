What Happens Next

The Road to Your Suite

We are actively in the process of securing the ideal space for NOVA Collective in Louisville. Here's what the timeline looks like and what to expect along the way:

Pre-Construction Phase (3–6 months)

  • Property search and Letter of Intent (LOI)
  • Lease drafting and negotiation with TI allowances
  • Architectural plans and city permitting

Build-Out Phase (3–4 months)

  • Framing, plumbing, and electrical for each private suite
  • Flooring, paint, lighting, cabinetry, and suite doors
  • City safety and fire inspections

Move-In Window

  • You'll receive a firm move-in estimate 60–90 days before opening
  • Pre-furnished suites will be ready with shampoo bowls, chairs, and custom finishes so you can open your doors to clients on Day One

The full process from securing the building to grand opening typically takes 6–10 months. We will keep you updated every step of the way.
